# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and evidence, never people.
- Protect private information and use only synthetic security examples.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and correct mistakes openly.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publication of another person's private
information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when
behaviour threatens a safe, professional community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer. Good-faith reports will be handled with discretion and without retaliation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted
from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
